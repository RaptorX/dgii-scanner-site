Privacy policy

DGII Scanner is operated by Isaias Baez. Contact graptorx@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Information and purposes

  • Account information: email, display name, sign-in provider, account identifier, verification status, and authentication credentials are processed through Google Firebase Authentication to provide sign-in, security, verification, and recovery. Google sign-in supplies basic identity information; the app does not request access to your Gmail messages, Drive files, or contacts.
  • Subscription records: account identity, plan, status, and relevant lifecycle timestamps are stored in Cloud Firestore to track account entitlements. Purchases are not currently enabled and the app does not collect payment-card details.
  • Receipt and profile information: receipt photos, extracted fields, amounts, supplier details, and personal profile details you enter are stored on your phone. Receipt images are processed locally for text and QR recognition. Account sign-in does not provide cloud receipt backup or cross-device receipt synchronization.

Network services

Firebase and Google process account requests and associated technical information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure their services. See Firebase privacy information and Google’s privacy policy. RNC directory updates download public supplier records from DGII. Where an online DGII receipt check is enabled, the receipt’s QR verification parameters are sent to the verification service and DGII; the full photo is not needed for that check.

Sharing and permissions

Camera and photo access support receipt capture and selection. Saving or sharing an export sends the selected content to the location or app you choose. We do not sell personal information or use receipt content for advertising. This website contains no analytics scripts, advertising, sign-in forms, or tracking cookies added by us. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical request logs under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Account and subscription information is retained while needed to provide the account. Use Account management → Delete account to request account deletion. The app removes the active account’s local receipt storage and protected photo keys during its deletion flow. A minimal account-deletion marker may remain to prevent the deleted account from being restored accidentally; service-provider logs and backups may follow their own retention periods. Exports or copies shared outside the app are not deleted by this action.

You can also request account deletion by emailing graptorx@gmail.com from your account email. We may verify ownership before acting. Uninstalling or clearing app data can remove local receipts without deleting your cloud account. Keep exports of records you need.

Your choices and security

You can review and update supported account fields, change or reset your password, sign out, revoke camera/photo permission through Android settings, and request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us. Local receipt storage uses encryption and account separation; no storage or transmission method is guaranteed completely secure. The app is intended for adults managing their own receipts and is not directed to children.

Changes

We will publish policy changes here with an updated effective date. Material changes to data handling will be communicated through the app or account contact where appropriate.
